How EzParts works

EzParts connects farmers with agricultural-parts dealers. Search for the right part, compare dealers on price, distance, and delivery, then order it. The dealer fulfills the order by shipping it to your farm or holding it for pickup. You pay the dealer's listed price; EzParts earns a small commission from the dealer — never added to your price.

Finding the right part

  • Search by part number, name, or description.
  • Search by machine — add the equipment you own and browse parts that fit.
  • Scan a part with your camera to identify it.

Orders, returns & refunds

  • Unused, uninstalled parts may be returned within 30 days in their original packaging.
  • Wrong or defective part? We'll arrange a free return and a replacement or full refund.
  • Core charges are refunded once the old core is returned to the dealer.
  • Refunds go to your original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days of the dealer receiving the return.
  • Returns are handled through the dealer that fulfilled your order; email us and we'll coordinate it.

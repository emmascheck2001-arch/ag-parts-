EzParts Privacy Policy

Effective June 2026 · Last updated August 2026

EzParts is a marketplace that connects farmers with agricultural-parts dealers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with.

Information we collect

  • Account & contact details — your name, email, and phone number.
  • Order & delivery information — for shipped orders, your delivery address.
  • Machines you add — the equipment you own, so we can match the right parts.
  • Location — with your permission, your approximate location to show nearby dealers and in-stock parts. You can decline this and still use the app.
  • Usage data — searches and interactions, used to improve fitment accuracy and the app.

How we use it

We use your information only to operate the service: to find parts that fit your machines, show dealers near you, process and fulfill your orders, and provide support.

Who we share it with

  • The fulfilling dealer — the parts dealer you order from receives the details needed to fulfill and ship your order.
  • Our payment processor (Stripe) — to process payments securely. EzParts does not store your full card details.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

Data retention

We keep order records as needed to provide the service, support returns, and meet legal and tax obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

Your choices

You can decline location access, update your details in the app, and request access to or deletion of your data by emailing us.
